Research

Publications

Journal articles

  • Benders-based solution strategies for stochastic capacity expansion models with loss of load probability constraints

    A. A. Albeladi and B. D. Leibowicz. Applied Energy, 2026. Article

    Summary

    A two-stage stochastic capacity-expansion model that embeds a loss-of-load probability requirement directly as a chance constraint, together with scenario-wise Benders strategies and structure-informed scenario selection that make the model tractable.

Working papers

  • Coordination problems and incentive pass-through in carbon capture, utilization, and storage development

    A. A. Albeladi and B. D. Leibowicz. Working paper.

    Runner-up, Dennis J. O'Brien Best Student Paper Competition, USAEE/IAEE 2024
    Summary

    A mixed-complementarity equilibrium model of capturers, traders, pipelines, storers, and utilizers. The paper asks how coordination failures change deployment and how policy incentives are transmitted through the CCUS value chain.

  • Optimal policy portfolios for carbon capture

    Methods: Stackelberg games, analytical optimization, policy design

    Details

    A leader–follower model of carbon-capture policy design. The policymaker chooses a portfolio of upfront support, output-based incentives, and emissions penalties while heterogeneous firms choose adoption and capture decisions. The goal is to understand when portfolios outperform single-instrument policy.

Ongoing projects

  • Learning-guided scenario selection for stochastic optimization

    Methods: Learning to rank, stochastic MILP, Benders decomposition

    Details

    Can a model learn which uncertainty scenarios are most likely to drive feasibility or computational difficulty? This project combines scenario-level features, ranking models, and optimization-based audits to guide reduced stochastic programs and decomposition algorithms without replacing mathematical optimization.

  • Resource adequacy beyond deterministic reserve margins

    Application: Power systems, capacity planning, reliability

    Details

    Extensions of the capacity-expansion framework that add transmission, battery state of charge, scenario reduction, and deterministic planning-reserve-margin benchmarks. The central question is how planning decisions change when adequacy is represented probabilistically inside the optimization model.

  • Strategic infrastructure cooperation under disruption risk

    Methods: Network optimization, cooperative games, risk scenarios

    Details

    Optimization and cooperative-game models for shared infrastructure when regions face correlated disruption risk. The work studies system value, coalition incentives, and allocation rules for large cross-border infrastructure investments.
