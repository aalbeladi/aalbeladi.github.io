Abdullah A. Albeladi · Benjamin D. Leibowicz

Current research threads

01

Optimal policy portfolios for carbon capture

A leader–follower model of carbon-capture policy design. The policymaker chooses a portfolio of upfront support, output-based incentives, and emissions penalties while heterogeneous firms choose adoption and capture decisions. The objective is to understand when portfolios outperform single-instrument policy.

MethodsStackelberg game
Analytical optimization

02

Learning-guided scenario selection for stochastic optimization

Can a model learn which uncertainty scenarios are most likely to drive feasibility or computational difficulty? This project combines scenario-level features, ranking models, and optimization-based audits to guide reduced stochastic programs and decomposition algorithms without replacing mathematical optimization.

MethodsLearning-to-rank
Stochastic MILP

03

Resource adequacy beyond deterministic reserve margins

Extensions of the capacity-expansion framework examine transmission, battery state of charge, scenario reduction, and deterministic planning-reserve-margin benchmarks. The central question is how planning decisions change when adequacy is represented probabilistically inside the optimization model.

ApplicationPower systems
Capacity planning
Reliability
04

Strategic infrastructure cooperation under disruption risk

Optimization and cooperative-game models for shared infrastructure when regions face correlated disruption risk. The work studies system value, coalition incentives, and allocation rules for large cross-border infrastructure investments.

MethodsNetwork optimization
