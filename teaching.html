Teaching

Teach the decision, not just the equation.

My goal is for students to leave a course understanding what a model means, when it is useful, and what can go wrong when its assumptions are ignored.

Approach

I organize technical material around three questions: What decision are we making? What information do we have? What does the model leave out?

In the classroom

How I structure learning.

01

Intuition before notation

Start with the engineering decision and the tradeoff. Introduce the mathematics only after the purpose of the model is clear.

02

Assumptions made visible

Students should know what a model assumes, why that assumption is convenient, and how the answer can change when it fails.

03

Application throughout

Examples are drawn from infrastructure, energy, operations, and investment decisions so that methods are connected to realistic contexts.
