About

My work sits at the intersection of mathematical optimization, energy systems, and decision science. I develop models that make uncertainty and strategic interaction explicit—then design algorithms that keep those models computationally useful.

Research agenda

Questions I care about.

My research is method-driven but problem-oriented: the mathematics should expose a decision tradeoff that matters in practice.

01

Planning under uncertainty

Stochastic and chance-constrained optimization for long-term infrastructure decisions, especially when reliability requirements must be enforced directly rather than checked after the fact.

02

Algorithms for difficult models

Decomposition, scenario selection, and learning-guided methods for large stochastic programs where computational burden is driven by a small set of structurally difficult scenarios.

03

Markets, incentives & coordination

Game-theoretic and equilibrium models that study how policy incentives move through infrastructure supply chains and how coordination failures alter investment and welfare.

04

Energy & infrastructure systems

Applications in power-system planning, resource adequacy, carbon capture and storage, environmental policy, and other capital-intensive systems exposed to uncertainty.

Selected publication

Reliability belongs inside the planning model.

Applied Energy · 2026

Benders-based solution strategies for stochastic capacity expansion models with loss of load probability constraints

With Benjamin D. Leibowicz. We formulate a two-stage stochastic capacity-expansion model that embeds a loss-of-load probability requirement directly as a chance constraint, and develop scenario-wise Benders strategies to make the resulting model tractable.
